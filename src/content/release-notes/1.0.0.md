## Welcome to Silktone v1

Hold your shortcut, speak, and Silktone types what you said into any app. Everything runs on your computer.

- **Five hand-picked models** — NVIDIA Parakeet, Nemotron and Canary, Cohere Transcribe and Whisper Medium. Pick one in **Models**.
- **Live streaming** — Parakeet Unified and Nemotron show your words as you speak.
- **New look** — the Silktone mark appears on screen while you talk and pulses while your text is prepared.
