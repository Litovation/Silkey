## Welcome to Silktone v0.1

Hold your shortcut, speak, and Silktone types what you said into any app. Everything runs on your computer.

- **Two NVIDIA models** — Nemotron Streaming 3.5 (live, 28 languages) and Canary 180M Flash (tiny and fast). Pick one in **Models**.
- **Live streaming** — Nemotron shows your words as you speak.
- **New look** — the Silktone mark appears on screen while you talk and pulses while your text is prepared.
