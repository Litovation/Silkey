## Silktone v0.1.5

Cleaner words, fewer clicks, and a new look.

- **Cleaner text** — "um" and repeated words are dropped, spoken punctuation ("comma", "new line") becomes punctuation, numbers become digits, spoken lists become lists, and "actually, make that…" corrections are applied for you.
- **Snippets and Fix word** — say a short cue to insert saved text, and fix a misheard word once so Silktone learns it.
- **Voice AI** — edit selected text, draft a message, or ask a quick question by voice (turn it on in **Writing**).
- **Quality: Low, Medium, High** — pick the model by how accurate you need it, from the tray or **Models**.
- **The Silktone bar** — a small see-through bar waits at the edge of your screen. Click it to dictate. If you type a lot, it may suggest speaking instead, for 10 seconds at most.
- **New window** — a see-through window shape with a status ledge showing whether Silktone is ready, its quality, language and version.
- **Mic check** — setup now tests your microphone and sets the level for you.
